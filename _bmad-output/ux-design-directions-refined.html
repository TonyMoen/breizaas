Exploring Color Variations

V1-V5: Pink hero colors with various gold accent strategies on dark backgrounds.

V6 "Liggi": Directly inspired by the successful song cover - warm vintage gold with playful purple.

V7-V10: Exploring different background colors inspired by the Liggi cover's warm bedroom aesthetic:

  • V7: Warm brown background + cream cards (bedroom shadow tones)
  • V8: Amber-tinted dark + golden hour glow (lamp lighting)
  • V9: Light cream background (inverted light mode)
  • V10: Deep brown + amber/purple gradient accents (dramatic lighting)

V11 - RECOMMENDED HYBRID: Combines V10's warm brown background with V6's clean vintage styling. No gradients, no fancy effects - just clean, sophisticated warmth.

V12 - DEEP PLUM: Rich velvet nightclub luxury (#2d1b2e) - purple-tinted dark background that amplifies the playful purple accents. Bold and dramatic.

V13 - WINTER TWILIGHT: Norwegian midnight sky blue (#1a1f28) - cool twilight tones that evoke winter nights. Sophisticated and atmospheric.
